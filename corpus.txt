BM25 is a ranking function used by search engines to score documents against a query.
Term frequency counts how often a word appears in a document.
Inverse document frequency gives rare words more weight than common words.
Document length normalization stops long documents from dominating the ranking.
FastAPI serves machine learning models over HTTP.
Dense retrieval encodes queries and documents as embeddings.
Hybrid search combines keyword scores with vector similarity.
Chunking splits long documents into passages before indexing.
